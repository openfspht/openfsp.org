---
title: Verify an attestation
description: Local verification of an attestation signature and, optionally, the digest of its associated report.
---

After an execution, the validator can produce a signed attestation in compact JWS format (EdDSA). Its payload contains the target, suite version and verdicts. Verification takes place in the browser using the key published by the validator at `/.well-known/openfsp-validator`. If the report is supplied, its SHA-256 digest, computed from its RFC 8785 canonical JSON representation, is compared with the digest recorded in the attestation.

The form uses the public validator [validator.openfsp.org](https://validator.openfsp.org) by default. To verify an attestation issued by another validator, enter that validator’s HTTPS address.

The attestation records execution results, including failures. It proves their origin and integrity for the key that signed it; it does not certify the implementation ([conformance 7.6](/en/docs/spec/conformance#7.6)).
