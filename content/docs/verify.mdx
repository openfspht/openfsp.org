---
title: Verify an attestation
description: Check the signature of a conformance attestation, and optionally the digest of its report, in your browser.
---

A conformance run signs an attestation: a compact JWS (EdDSA) whose payload records the target, the suite version and the verdicts. The signature is checked here, in your browser, against the key the validator publishes at `/.well-known/openfsp-validator`. The report file is optional: when given, its SHA-256 digest (RFC 8785 canonical JSON) is compared with the one in the attestation.

The public validator is not online yet. For an attestation produced locally, enter the address of your validator: `http://localhost:5176` with `make demo`, `http://localhost:8095` with `make dev`.

An attestation records one run, failures included. It does not certify an implementation ([conformance 7.6](/docs/spec/conformance#7.6)).
